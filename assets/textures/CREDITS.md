# Texture credits

All surface textures in this folder are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
and are released under **CC0** (public domain). They were downloaded at 1K
resolution and re-compressed (roughness maps downsized to 512 px).

| Folder | Poly Haven asset | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lichen_rock/` | [Lichen Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/lichen_rock) | cliffs and crags (triplanar) |
| `sparse_grass/` | [Sparse Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass) | the Deeping Coomb's turf |
| `muddy_tracks/` | [Muddy Tracks](https://polyhaven.com/a/muddy_tracks) | ground trampled by the host |
| `rocky_trail/` | [Rocky Trail](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail) | roads, paths, courtyard |
| `rock_wall_08/` | [Rock Wall 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_wall_08) | Hornburg and Deeping Wall masonry |
| `medieval_wood/` | [Medieval Wood](https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_wood) | the great gate |
| `weathered_planks/` | [Weathered Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_planks) | roofs, ladders, the horn stand |
